### About Me

I'm currently pursuing a **B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science** at RVS Technical Campus (2024–2028). I build full-stack web applications and enjoy taking them a step further with 3D visualization and AI-assisted features — projects like an interactive 3D car configurator, an AI-assisted research paper formatter, and an exam platform with a proctoring concept all come from that instinct: pick a real, slightly boring workflow and make it faster or more usable.

I'm early in my AI/ML journey — actively learning machine learning, data analysis, and Python — and my long-term direction is toward AI engineering: building products where machine learning and large language models are a core part of the system, not just a feature label.

I enjoy problems that mix a practical UI with an underlying technical challenge: real-time coordination (food redistribution), spatial/3D interaction (the car showroom), or workflow automation (the research paper assistant).
